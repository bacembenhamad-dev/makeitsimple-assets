Privacy Policy

makeitsimple.tech is an educational Instagram account that publishes explainers, book recommendations and free tools for people who work in IT. It is operated by one person, Bacem Ben Hamad, in Tunisia.

This policy covers the automation used to run that account, including the Meta app makeitsimple.tech, which posts content and answers comments.

What the app does

  • Publishes posts to the makeitsimple.tech Instagram account.
  • Reads public comments left on those posts.
  • When a comment contains the keyword shown on a post, it sends that commenter a single private reply containing the link that was offered publicly in the post.
  • Posts a short public reply so the commenter knows the message has been sent.

What is stored

Only what is needed to avoid messaging the same person twice for the same comment:

  • the comment identifier,
  • the public Instagram username that wrote it,
  • the time the reply was sent.

This is held in a file on a private machine controlled by the operator. No email addresses, phone numbers, payment details or message history are collected.

What is not done

  • Data is never sold, rented or shared with any third party.
  • No advertising, profiling or tracking.
  • No marketing sequence. One comment produces exactly one message, containing the requested link and nothing else.
  • No message is sent to anyone who has not commented asking for it.

How long it is kept

Records older than 90 days are deleted, as they serve no further purpose.

Your choices

You can delete your comment at any time. To ask what is held about you, or to have it removed, email the address below and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Third parties

The account runs on Instagram, so Meta Platforms processes the data involved under Instagram's own privacy policy. Published images are served from GitHub Pages. No analytics or advertising services are used.

Children

The account is intended for adults working or studying in technology, and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
